# ChatGPT Review Analysis

## Overview
This project focuses on analyzing **customer reviews of ChatGPT** to gain insights into user satisfaction and experience. The objective is to perform a **comprehensive sentiment analysis** and **feature extraction** from textual review data, highlighting patterns of positive and critical feedback.

## Key Objectives
- Understand the **overall sentiment** expressed by ChatGPT users.  
- Measure the **strength and subjectivity** of sentiments.  
- Identify the **features or aspects** of ChatGPT most frequently praised or criticized.  
- Provide actionable insights to inform product improvements and user engagement strategies.

## Features & Methodology
- **Data Preprocessing:** Cleaning and preparing textual review data for analysis.  
- **Sentiment Analysis:** Scoring reviews to classify them as positive, negative, or neutral.  
- **Keyword & Feature Extraction:** Identifying frequently mentioned features and keywords.  
- **Visualizations:** Creating charts and graphs to display sentiment distribution, feature frequency, and trends.

## Technologies Used
- **Python** – Data processing, sentiment analysis, and visualization  
- **Pandas & NumPy** – Data manipulation  
- **NLTK / TextBlob / spaCy** – Natural Language Processing  
- **Matplotlib & Seaborn** – Visualizations  

## Impact
This analysis provides a clear understanding of **user opinions and experiences** with ChatGPT, helping stakeholders identify areas of strength and potential improvement in the product.
